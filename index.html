Our Story

From a small kitchen to a beloved table.

Maison Olive began with a single wood oven, a stack of borrowed cookbooks, and a stubborn belief that the best meals are unhurried.

Today, Chef Elena Marchetti and her team source from twenty-three small farms across the Hudson Valley, building a menu that changes with the light of the season.
